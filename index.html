Frequently asked questions

How does annual billing work?

You pay for 10 months and get 12. The per-month price shown is the monthly price times 10, divided by 12.

Which currencies can I see prices in?

PKR, USD and GBP. Rates are fixed on this page: 1 USD = 278.50 PKR and 1 GBP = 355.00 PKR.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, you can move up a plan at any time. The new plan starts on your next billing date.

Is there root access?

Every plan includes full root access, so you can install what you need.

Is this a real product?

No. This is an assessment demo with invented plans and prices.
